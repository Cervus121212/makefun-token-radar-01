The formula

implied = TVL × category multiple × growth mult × age mult × chain mult

Category multiple
Median market-cap / TVL of the live-token protocols in the same category (3 or more comps), else the global median. The market sets the price; the model only reads it.
Growth mult
1 + clamp(7-day change, −50%, +100%) ÷ 100 × 0.6. A protocol growing fast is worth more than a shrinking one at the same TVL.
Age mult
0.70 + 0.30 × min(age in days, 730) ÷ 730. Older protocols have survived longer; unknown age scores 0.85.
Chain mult
1 + 0.02 × min(chains − 1, 5). More chains, more surface for users and for a launch.
